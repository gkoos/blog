The Database Safari

Exploring Vector, Time-Series, Graph, Columnar/OLAP, and Geospatial Databases in the Wild

The Database Safari is a practical field guide to the wild world beyond traditional SQL and NoSQL. Journey through columnar, lakehouse, event-stream, time-series, probabilistic, vector, graph, and geospatial databases to discover how each is engineered, where it excels, and what trade-offs it brings. Grounded in real-world workloads and architectural patterns, the book cuts through the hype to help developers, architects, and data engineers recognize when a general-purpose database is enough—and when a specialist has truly earned its place. The result is a clear, workload-first guide to building intentional hybrid data architectures that are fast, scalable, and operationally sustainable.
